Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty **The Houseplant Out-of-Office**: the funniest out-of-office auto-reply a houseplant would send, 120 characters or fewer.

## Entry

> Out of office until someone waters me. Last watered: March. Your email is in line behind the sun.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only) writes the one-line auto-reply to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical output.
